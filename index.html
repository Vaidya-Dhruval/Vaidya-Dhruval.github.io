01 —— Research · Thesis

Whole-body manipulation, learned from data and demonstration.

Writing report · Defense July 2026

Whole-Body Manipulation & Navigation using Reinforcement Learning & Behavior Cloning

Mobile manipulators face a fundamental coordination problem: the base and the arm are usually controlled separately, leaving performance — and reachability — on the table. My thesis investigates learning-based whole-body control, where the entire mobile-manipulator system reasons about navigation and manipulation as a single coupled task.

The work combines Reinforcement Learning (for exploration and policy optimization in simulation) with Behavior Cloning (for sample-efficient learning from human demonstrations) — an approach designed for the kind of unstructured environments real industrial robots actually live in.
